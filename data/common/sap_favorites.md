# Managing SAP Favorites — Drive Medical

Favorites speed up daily work by giving you one-click access to the
transactions or Fiori apps you use most.

## SAP GUI (ECC) — Favorites Menu
1. Right-click the **Favorites** folder in the SAP Easy Access menu.
2. Choose **Insert Transaction**.
3. Type the transaction code (e.g. `ME21N`) and press Enter.
4. Optionally rename the entry by right-clicking → **Change favorite**.

You can also organize favorites into folders (right-click → **Insert Folder**)
— Drive Medical recommends one folder per business process (e.g. `AP`,
`Procurement`, `Reporting`).

## Exporting / Importing Favorites
- **Export**: Favorites menu → **Download to PC** (saves an `.SFV` file).
- **Import**: Favorites menu → **Upload from PC**.

This is useful when you get a new laptop or join a new team and want a
colleague's favorites as a starting point.

## SAP Fiori (S/4HANA) — My Home & Pinning Tiles
1. Open the Fiori Launchpad.
2. Click the **Edit Home Page** pencil icon (top right).
3. Click **+ Add Section** to create a section like *"Daily Tasks"*.
4. Click **Add Apps** and pick from the catalog filtered to your role.
5. Drag tiles between sections to reorder.
6. Click **Save**.

## Searching Apps
Use the magnifying glass at the top of the Fiori shell to search by app name
or transaction code. Searching for `MIRO` will surface the **Supplier
Invoice** Fiori app.
